Your rights, costs and contacts

Taking part is your choice
Taking part in this research study is voluntary. You can say no, and saying no will not change your regular medical care or any benefits you would otherwise get. Once the sensor is implanted, you cannot leave the study until it is removed at the day 180 visit.

Will it cost me anything?
Isbrann Medical AS provides the sensor, the transmitter and the study app at no cost to you. You and your health insurance will not be billed for study visits or study tests. You or your insurance will still pay for your regular diabetes care, such as insulin and supplies.

Will I be paid?
To make up for your time and travel, you will receive $50 for each of the 8 regular clinic visits and $150 for each of the 3 long clinic visits, up to $850 in total. If you leave the study early, you keep the payments for the visits you completed.

Who will see my information?
We will keep your study records private as far as the law allows. Your records will carry a study number instead of your name. Isbrann Medical AS and its monitors, the Institutional Review Board (IRB) and the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) may look at your records to check that the study is done correctly.

Will I be told about new information?
Yes. We will tell you about any new findings during the study that might change your mind about staying in it.

Whom can I contact?
For questions about the study, or if you think the study has hurt you, call the VARDE-1 study line at 1-555-0142. It is answered 24 hours a day. For questions about your rights as a research participant, call Kestrel Point IRB at 1-555-0177.

Signing this form
Take as much time as you need to decide. You can take this form home and talk about it with your family or your own doctor. If you agree to take part, sign and date this form. You will get a signed and dated copy to keep.
