PART 1. INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE
==================================================

FlexiGuard(TM) FG-200
Silicone Foam Wound Dressing
Sterile. Single use. Rx only.


1. PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

FlexiGuard FG-200 is a sterile, single-use, three-layer foam dressing:
(a) a perforated soft silicone wound contact layer that holds the dressing gently on intact skin around the wound without sticking to the moist wound bed;
(b) an absorbent polyurethane foam pad that takes up wound exudate;
(c) a vapour-permeable polyurethane film backing that keeps out liquids and allows moisture vapour to escape, so the wound bed stays moist.

The dressing absorbs exudate while maintaining a moist wound environment. It contains no drugs, biologics or antimicrobial agents. It is not made with natural rubber latex.


2. SIZES AND VARIANTS

FG-200-05: dressing 5 x 5 cm, foam pad 3.5 x 3.5 cm, 10 dressings per box, green colour band.
FG-200-10: dressing 10 x 10 cm, foam pad 7.5 x 7.5 cm, 10 dressings per box, blue colour band.
FG-200-15: dressing 15 x 15 cm, foam pad 12 x 12 cm, 10 dressings per box, purple colour band.

Choose a size where the foam pad overlaps the wound edge by at least 2 cm on all sides.


3. INTENDED USE

FlexiGuard FG-200 is intended for the management of partial-thickness wounds.


4. INDICATIONS FOR USE

FlexiGuard FG-200 is indicated for adult patients for the management of pressure ulcers (category 2) and venous leg ulcers. The dressing is for use under the direction of a healthcare professional.


5. CONTRAINDICATIONS

Do not use FlexiGuard FG-200:
(a) on third-degree (full-thickness) burns;
(b) on patients with known sensitivity to silicone or to any of the dressing materials.


6. WARNINGS

W1. Sterile unless the pouch is opened or damaged. Do not use if the pouch is open, torn or damaged, or if the use-by date has passed.
W2. Single use only. Do not reuse. Reuse may cause infection or cross-contamination, and the dressing may not perform as intended.
W3. Do not resterilize.
W4. Not intended for third-degree burns or for wounds with exposed bone, tendon or muscle.
W5. Inspect the skin around the wound at each dressing change. If the skin is white, soft or wrinkled (signs of maceration), change the dressing more often or consult the treating clinician.
W6. Stop using the dressing and consult a physician if redness, itching, rash or other signs of skin sensitivity or allergic reaction occur.
W7. The dressing does not treat infection. Inspect the wound at each change. If signs of infection appear (increased pain, redness, swelling, heat, odour or pus), consult a physician. The dressing may be used during treatment of an infection only on the advice of a physician.


7. PRECAUTIONS

W8. Do not use together with oxidizing solutions such as hypochlorite or hydrogen peroxide, which can damage the foam.
W9. For venous leg ulcers, use the dressing under the compression therapy prescribed by the treating clinician. Do not apply compression to patients with an ankle-brachial pressure index below 0.8 unless directed by a physician.
W10. For pressure ulcers, continue pressure redistribution. The dressing does not replace pressure relief.
(a) Do not stretch the dressing during application.
(b) The dressing may be left in place during showering if the film backing is intact and edges are sealed.
(c) Safety and effectiveness have not been established in children under 18 years or in pregnant or breastfeeding women.


8. INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE

1. Wash your hands and put on clean gloves.
2. Cleanse the wound with sterile saline or a non-oxidizing wound cleanser according to local protocol. Dry the surrounding skin thoroughly.
3. Choose the dressing size so that the foam pad overlaps the wound edge by at least 2 cm on all sides (see the size table in section 2).
4. Check that the pouch is intact and that the use-by date has not passed.
5. Open the pouch at the chevron and remove the dressing without touching the silicone side.
6. Remove the release liners.
7. Place the dressing silicone side down, centred over the wound. Do not stretch it. Smooth the border from the centre outwards.
8. For venous leg ulcers, apply the prescribed compression bandage or stocking over the dressing.
9. Inspect the dressing daily if possible. Change the dressing when exudate reaches within 1 cm of the pad edge, when the dressing leaks or lifts, or at least every 7 days. Inspect the wound and surrounding skin at every change.
10. To remove, lift one corner and peel the dressing slowly back over itself in the direction of hair growth. If it sticks, moisten it with sterile saline before removal.
11. Dispose of the used dressing as clinical waste according to local requirements.


9. STERILITY, SHELF LIFE AND STORAGE

Sterilized using ethylene oxide. Sterile unless the package is opened or damaged.
Shelf life: 3 years from the date of manufacture. The use-by date is printed on each pouch and carton.
Storage: store between 5 °C and 30 °C (41 °F and 86 °F) in a dry place, away from direct sunlight. Do not freeze.


10. REPORTING

Report any serious incident or adverse event involving this device to the manufacturer and to FDA MedWatch.
