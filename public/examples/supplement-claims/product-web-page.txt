What the research says
Stillnora Evening Calm was tested in a 4-week randomized, placebo-controlled study in 64 healthy adults aged 25 to 60. Participants who took 2 capsules each evening reported better sleep quality on the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index than those who took a placebo.

How to use
Adults take 2 capsules with water 30 to 60 minutes before bedtime. Do not take more than 2 capsules in 24 hours. Ask your healthcare provider before use if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication or have a medical condition. Not for use by persons under 18 years of age.
