Device description
The FlexiGuard wound dressing (model FG-200) is made of a silicone contact layer and polyurethane foam. Principle of operation: it absorbs exudate while keeping the wound moist.

Intended use
Intended use: management of partial-thickness wounds. Indications for use: adults with pressure ulcers and venous leg ulcers. Contraindications: third-degree burns.

Clinical evidence
A prospective clinical study with 148 subjects reported 2 device-related adverse events through 12 months of follow-up.

Biocompatibility
Testing per ISO 10993-1 included cytotoxicity and sensitization.

Labeling
The label includes warnings and instructions for use.

Substantial equivalence
The predicate device is K123456.
